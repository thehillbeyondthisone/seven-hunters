A reconstruction study. Short, fine, wind-exposed grass and muted winter colour are art direction for the coast, not a measured botanical reconstruction of 1901.

The game’s vegetation and saves are unchanged. No project has been committed or adopted.

What is actually running?

Field Grass calls the upstream scatter, tuft geometry, TSL material, and interaction field directly. Terra calls the upstream grass mesh generator and both GLSL shaders directly, with its original Three.js runtime. The common test scene, shorter/narrower blades, procedural ground textures, and controls were written for this comparison. No original Terra artwork or audio is used.

Source revisions, file references, and integration limits are in the study notes. These samples do not measure the main game’s performance.
