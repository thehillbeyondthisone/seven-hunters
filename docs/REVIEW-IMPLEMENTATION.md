## History and authored weather

Walter, Cate, the solitary 3 January posting, the later company promise and January landing destruction are fiction. NLB's contemporary record places replacement keepers ashore on **29 December 1900**. Muirhead's report was submitted **8 January 1901** and describes the December inspection; it is not evidence for a January destruction event. Existing December west-landing damage remains distinct from the authored storm sequence. [Northern Lighthouse Board accounts](https://www.nlb.org.uk/history/flannan-isles/).

The independent research inspected Met Office January/February 1901 reports. These are **regional Stornoway observations, not measurements on Eilean Mòr**. They cannot establish exact island visibility, wave height or a relief promise. Examples relevant to review: 3–4 January have regional wind/cloud/rain observations; 12 January has blue-sky observations that support a daylight photography setting; 18 January has overcast observations without a fog code; 22 January has strong WNW/NNW winds, rather than calm; the 27–28 January period has strong regional gale/sea evidence and 28 January regional snow observations. None verifies exact Flannan fog or the fictional destructive wave. No unrequested snow, date or narrative rewrite was added. [Met Office scanned reports](https://digital.nmla.metoffice.gov.uk/IO_12467daa-ce3d-4a34-b942-a9cb8eb6d0e5/), [CEDA collection provenance](https://catalogue.ceda.ac.uk/uuid/235ff4a040854dcd8dfb754bbb898479/).

A contemporary NLB report mentions a powerful telescope used from the mainland. That establishes period availability, not a documented telescope issued at Flannan; the existing in-game scope remains an authored reconstruction.

The Brownie's supported indoor time exposure is grounded in the original Eastman Kodak January 1900 instructions, printed pages 13–17. The comfortable hold duration in the game is a gameplay abstraction, not an exposure-meter simulation. No flash or modern film rule was invented. [Original instructions scan](https://manuals.plus/m/ad159776d5786e3094c8bdc6c3c72eeabcd3de73de75beb3e8b1b8069ac8fd0a_optim.pdf).
