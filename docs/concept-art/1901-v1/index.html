Source register

1. Northern Lighthouse Board — contemporary accounts

Moore's 28 December 1900 letter and Muirhead's 8 January 1901 report: working objects, orderly station, landings and clothing.

2. Historic Environment Scotland — LB48143

Architectural type, flat-roofed house, diagonal lantern glazing, cast-iron staircase and enclosure. This later survey includes modern changes.

3. Museum of Scottish Lighthouses — 1899 prism

Museum object SLM.1997.9316 confirms the original hyper-radial optic.

4. US Lighthouse Society — Hyper-Radial Lenses

Henry-Lepaute bivalve optic description and original photograph, courtesy Museum of Scottish Lighthouses.

5. National Records of Scotland — Flannan keepers

Modern exterior photo © Ian Cowe and circa-1900 keeper group photo. Modern photo supports massing, not 1901 fittings.

6. Northern Lighthouse Board — station details

23-metre tower and 101-metre focal elevation. Current equipment specifications do not describe the original lens.

7. HES — kitchen and roof drawing DP 038541

1896 drawing, original DC 8666B; C/32/9. Catalogue read, full drawing unavailable in public viewer.

8. HES — station collection

DC 8664: site; DC 8665: station; DC 8666A/B: house; DC 8667–8672: lantern and fixtures. Full drawings still need examination.
