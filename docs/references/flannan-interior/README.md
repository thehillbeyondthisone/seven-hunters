# Actual Flannan interior references

Prepared 7 October 2026 for the game's 3 January 1901 setting. Open [the visual reference board](index.html). `references.json` records each image's source, reported date, useful observations and limits; `file-manifest.json` records dimensions and SHA-256 hashes.

This pack contains four photographs identified by their published sources as the actual station interior, two scans of original lantern engineering drawings, and the museum-attributed photograph of the original 1899 optic retained from the earlier research pack. All images are unchanged local study copies. No generated concepts are included, and no game code has been changed by this reference-gathering task.

## What the photographs establish

The later occupied station had deep window recesses, substantial masonry walls, simple shared furniture, boarded ceilings and timber finishes in at least one photographed space. These observations provide useful construction and scale references. They do **not** prove which room had which finish in 1901, an exact furniture layout, paint colours or a measured floor plan.

The two small colour images are captioned 1969–1971 by the download mirror. The two black-and-white room images are captioned circa 1971 there; related interior photographs on Keith McCloskey's indexed page are dated to the 1960s and credited to Ron Ireland. The author's live site did not resolve during retrieval, so the original pixel match, dates and attribution are still to be checked. Existing filenames containing `1960s` preserve the research lead, not a verified date.

Exclude the radios, electric kettle, electric lights, glass-block windows, modern upholstery and packaged food from the 1901 scene. The panelling, its colours, rugs and exact clock cannot yet be called original. The distressed wall surfaces in the later photographs are unsuitable as a finish target for the recently built station in 1901.

## Original construction and period evidence

The two engineering scans are explicitly titled for the Flannan lighthouse lantern. They show its plan/elevation, diagonal glazing, panels, portholes and metalwork details. The mirror calls them 1895 sheets. Their exact archive accession, date and scale need matching against the archive master; they are **not** domestic-room plans.

The original optic photograph remains the controlling lens reference: the 1899 Henry-Lepaute hyper-radial bivalve apparatus with paired bullseyes. It does not justify guessing machinery hidden outside the view.

[HES's building record](https://portal.historicenvironment.scot/designation/LB48143) provides architectural evidence for the cast-iron staircase with timber handrail and timber deck on iron girders. [The NLB contemporary reports](https://www.nlb.org.uk/history/flannan-isles/) establish a kitchen fireplace, beds, cleaned utensils, clocks, slate and working records. They do not supply a full furnishing inventory.

## Remaining archive gap

[Trove DP 038541](https://www.trove.scot/image/1104665) identifies DC 8666B / C/32/9, dated 16 May 1896, covering kitchen, bedroom and coal-cellar sections and the roof. The public viewer presented an access check; the full sheet has **not** been obtained or inspected. The [station archive](https://www.trove.scot/place/171215) also identifies DC 8665 and DC 8666A/B for the house/tower. Until those drawings can be read, the game room layout and exact assignments remain a reconstruction.

Do not substitute the Breasclete mainland family-house drawings, the island chapel interior, another lighthouse, a film set or generated artwork for Eilean Mor's domestic rooms.

## Direction for the approved interior pass

1. Use the real photographs to guide masonry depth, window reveals, timber construction and the practical scale of furniture. Preserve existing navigation, story interactions and unpacking.
2. Support 1901 working objects with the contemporary reports; use dated object references for any newly modelled stove, lamp, bed or utensils. Label unresolved furniture and finish choices as reconstruction.
3. Keep the station orderly and maintained. Treat the earlier generated kitchen as an atmospheric study, not the factual template.
4. Use the original optic photograph and lantern sheets for apparatus and metalwork. Compare the isolated workroom preview in day and night before expanding the pass.

Source photographs retain their original rights and credits. This pack remains local reference material, separate from game textures and published assets.
