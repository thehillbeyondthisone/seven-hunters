# Signal lamp references

The walkway signal lamp is modelled on the Begbie-pattern oil signalling lamp ("Lamp, Signalling, B"),
the British Army's standard flashing lamp from the mid-1880s until the First World War. Source model:
`tools/props/signal-lamp.blend` (export with its `export_signal_lamp` text block → `src/world/flannan/SignalLampModel.js`).

Photographs used (not copied here; see the links):

- Collections WA, "Pre 1914, Begbie Signal Lamp 1885 - 1915": black-painted steel box about 38 cm tall, convex
  lens, shutters worked by a knob on top, brass and galvanised fittings.
  https://collectionswa.net.au/items/4cc23cb3-5ea2-46f7-8b9e-e66ccfb941ae
- Soldiers of Oxfordshire Museum, Begbie oil signal lamp: 1880s, lens-focused beam, shutter knob on top.
  https://www.sofo.org.uk/exhibits/begbie-oil-signal-lamp/
- International Military Antiques, Canadian WWI-era Begbie lamp with tripod and transit chest: front and side
  views on the wooden tripod, the chimney cap and handle on top.
  https://ima-usa.com/products/original-canadian-wwi-era-lamp-signaling-b-begbie-signal-lamp-with-original-transit-box-with-accessories-and-tripod-marked-13-cmr-for-13th-regiment-canadian-mounted-rifles
- Great War Forum, Begbie lamp variations (chimney extended or retracted).
  https://www.greatwarforum.org/topic/275127-variation-begbie-lamp/

Reconstructed rather than recorded: exact box proportions (about 20 x 24 x 28 cm), vane count, door and
fitting placement, and the tripod's height. Nothing found places a Begbie lamp at the Flannan station: it is the
period-correct choice for a portable shuttered signalling lamp in 1900, not a documented fitting.
