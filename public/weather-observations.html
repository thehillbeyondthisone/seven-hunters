Go out and look

Read the barometer in the keepers’ room and the shaded air thermometer outside the north wall. Look up at the cupola vane from the open yard: its arrow points into the wind.

On the balcony, look outward and down at open water. Hold the action for four seconds to judge the sea. Check Gallan Head’s bearing and the nearby island’s bearing, even when fog hides them. Return to the slate and chalk the readings you actually captured.
