SEVEN HUNTERS · DAY TWO

Go out and look

Read the barometer in the keepers’ room and the shaded air thermometer outside the north wall. Look up at the cupola vane from the open yard: its arrow points into the wind.

Step onto the balcony and look out over the sea. The pale timber rail marks a broad lookout. Hold the action for four seconds to judge the sea, then identify Gallan Head and Eilean Tighe. Their names remain available once identified. Return to the slate and chalk the readings you actually captured.
