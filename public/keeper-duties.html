SEVEN HUNTERS · FIRST-NIGHT STUDY

Keeping the evening light

A prepared lamp, a revolving optic, and the weight that drives it. Learn what each does by attending to the apparatus in the lantern.

Enter the lanternTouch controls

Examine the lamp. Raise the driving weight at the crank. At sunset, light the burner and release the brass clockwork stop beside the crank. Watch the flame settle and the optic turn, then examine it again.

On your first night, follow the stair down to the driving weight. Examine it, watch it descend on its cable, then return to the crank and raise it again while the optic keeps turning.
