SEVEN HUNTERS · THE FIRST WATCH

A room to keep watch in.

The climb is behind you. Shut the door against the wind. There are three chairs at the table, instructions on the desk, and a light to show before the sun goes down.

Begin at the houseTouch controls

Read your posting, take the storm lantern and climb to the apparatus. Examine the prepared lamp, raise the driving weight, light the burner and release the clockwork. Watch the glass turn around the steady flame.

Once the light is working, attend to its driving weight. Return to the room to unpack your bag and discover the Brownie Mary sent with you. Then keep watch for a reply from Gallan Head across the water.
